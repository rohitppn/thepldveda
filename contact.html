    Before You Call

    A few things to have ready.

    A short, focused first call leads to a better treatment plan. There is no rush — but these details help.

    • A clear description of your concern, and roughly how long you have had it
    • Any previous treatments you have tried (creams, hospitals, other doctors)
    • A list of any medications you currently take, including over-the-counter
    • Any major health conditions — heart, diabetes, thyroid, etc.
    • For vitiligo: photographs of the patches if you can share them on WhatsApp
    • A quiet space where you can speak openly for 15–20 minutes
