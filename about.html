    About the Doctor

    A practice rooted in listening, lineage, and the Vedas.

    For over two decades, Dr. Pravesh Rawat has been returning Ayurveda to its source — classical texts, hand-prepared remedies, and the patient at the centre of every decision.

    Her Story

    Ayurveda was the family lineage long before it was a degree.

    Dr. Pravesh Rawat grew up in a household where Ayurveda was the everyday medicine. Long before he trained formally, he had already learned to identify herbs by smell, to grind a paste at the right consistency, and to read a patient's prakriti from the way they sat down.

    That early exposure shaped a practice that refuses shortcuts. Every formulation is prepared by hand. Every diagnosis begins with a conversation, not a checklist. Every patient is treated as a whole person — not a condition to be matched against a product.

    Why The Old Veda

    Modern Ayurveda has, for many practitioners, become indistinguishable from over-the-counter wellness. Capsules on shelves, bottles with year-long shelf lives, the same formula sold to thousands. The classical texts — the Charaka Samhita, the Sushruta Samhita — never imagined this.

    The Old Veda is a return to that original method. Small batches. Personal consultation. Medicine that exists for one person at a time.

    What he treats

    Dr. Pravesh's clinical focus is on two areas where personalisation matters most: vitiligo (white patches on the skin), and private sexual health concerns where most patients have nowhere else to turn for an honest, judgement-free conversation.

    Her Approach

    Four guiding principles.

    Time over speed

    Healing is a process, not a transaction. Treatments are paced to your body's response, not a marketing calendar.

    The conversation is the diagnosis

    No questionnaire form. No call centre. The doctor herself listens, asks, and decides.

    Confidentiality, always

    What is shared with the doctor stays with the doctor. Especially for sexual health — your privacy is the foundation.

    The texts, not trends

    Every formulation traces back to classical Ayurvedic literature. No fad ingredients, no marketing-driven additions.
