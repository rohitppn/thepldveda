    Understanding Vitiligo

    Why patches appear, in the language of Ayurveda.

    In modern dermatology, vitiligo is described as the loss of melanocytes — the cells that give skin its colour. But that is the what, not the why. Classical Ayurveda calls this condition shvitra, and traces it to deeper imbalances: digestive fire (agni) gone weak, accumulated toxins (ama), and a disturbance in the body's regulating doshas — most often pitta.

    This is why creams alone don't hold. The body is producing the patch from within. Treatment that doesn't address the root simply waits for the next patch to appear. Vaidya ji's formulation works on both — internal cause and visible patch — at the same time.

    What to Expect

    Healing is slow, then sudden.

    Vitiligo treatment is measured in months, not days. The first sign is usually stabilisation — the spreading stops. Repigmentation follows, often beginning as small dark dots within the patch that gradually expand and merge.

    Most patients see meaningful change between 4–12 months, depending on how long they have had the condition and how consistently they follow the plan. The Vaidhya will give you a realistic timeline at the first consultation — never a guarantee, never a promise of weeks.
