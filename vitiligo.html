    Understanding Vitiligo

    Why patches appear, in the language of Ayurveda.

    In modern dermatology, vitiligo is described as the loss of melanocytes — the cells that give skin its colour. But that is the what, not the why. Classical Ayurveda calls this condition shvitra, and traces it to deeper imbalances: digestive fire (agni) gone weak, accumulated toxins (ama), and a disturbance in the body's regulating doshas — most often pitta.

    This is why creams alone don't hold. The body is producing the patch from within. Treatment that doesn't address the root simply waits for the next patch to appear.

    Our Treatment Approach

    Three movements toward repigmentation.

    Every patient's plan is unique — but every plan moves through the same three stages, in the right order.

    Read the prakriti

    A long, unhurried consultation. Dr. Pravesh reads your constitution, your history, your digestion, your sleep — anything that points toward the imbalance underneath the patch.

    Restore the internal balance

    Hand-prepared internal medicine to clear toxins, strengthen agni, and bring the disturbed dosha back to harmony. This is what stops new patches and prepares the body to repigment.

    Apply the topical formulation

    A classical paste prepared from herbs known for melanin support, applied directly to the patch. Used in combination with the internal medicine — never alone.

    What to Expect

    Healing is slow, then sudden.

    Vitiligo treatment is measured in months, not days. The first sign is usually stabilisation — the spreading stops. Repigmentation follows, often beginning as small dark dots within the patch that gradually expand and merge.

    Most patients see meaningful change between 4–12 months, depending on how long they have had the condition and how consistently they follow the plan. The doctor will give you a realistic timeline at the first consultation — never a guarantee, never a promise of weeks.

    Frequently Asked

    Questions patients often ask.

    Is Ayurvedic treatment for vitiligo permanent?
    When the underlying imbalance is corrected and the patient maintains a stable lifestyle, the results tend to hold. We are addressing the root cause, not just the visible patch — but Ayurveda also asks for ongoing care, not a one-time fix.
    How long does treatment usually take?
    Most patients see clear progress between 4 and 12 months. Older patches and larger areas take longer. The doctor will give you a personal timeline at your first call.
    Do I need to come to the clinic, or can this be done remotely?
    The first conversation can absolutely happen on the phone. For most cases, ongoing consultation is also done by phone or WhatsApp, and medicines are couriered to you. An in-person visit is welcome but not required.
    Are there side effects?
    The medicines are prepared from natural herbs in the classical method. Side effects are rare, but Dr. Pravesh will always discuss your existing medications and any health conditions before prescribing.
    Can I continue allopathic treatment alongside?
    In many cases, yes — and the doctor will guide you on what to keep, what to pause, and how to combine safely. Always disclose every medication you take.
    How much does the treatment cost?
    Costs vary because every formulation is personalised. The doctor will discuss this transparently on your first call — there are no hidden fees and no shelf products to upsell.
